The Sandy Experience

Sandy VaVoom is Central Coast vocalist Sandra Landeros Thomas. From intimate rooms to full-band nights, she builds every booking around strong communication, audience connection, and a curated setlist that fits the room, from the classic 1950s all the way to modern hits.

"Whether it is an intimate set or a full-band night, the promise is the same: people feel looked after."
